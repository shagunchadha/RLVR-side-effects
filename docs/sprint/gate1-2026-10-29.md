**What this shows**
- **The readout is valid.** B1-H5 holds: agreement is ≥ 0.96 in both checkpoints, well above 0.8. So B1-H3 and B1-H4 can be interpreted.
- **The extreme-answer shift replicates, under both sampling and the logprob readout.**
  - Under sampling, RLVR's extreme share is +18.1 points. The frozen run, at different sampling and with 3 seeds, had +17.8.
  - The readout gives +17.2 points.
- **The shift is not a sampling artefact.** Readout entropy is identical (0.08 in both): RLVR is not more uncertain, it puts its mass on the endpoints.
- **Thinking moves the answer away from the prior, and more for RLVR.** The no-think priors have the same mean (3.33 vs 3.33) and RLVR's is only slightly more extreme (0.125 vs 0.060). After thinking, RLVR's expected rating has moved 0.72 scale points from its prior on average, DPO's 0.50.
- **So most of the extreme shift is produced during reasoning,** about 0.11 of the 0.17 readout difference: 0.172 total minus 0.065 already present in the prior.
- **B1-H2 is borderline.** The bootstrap interval excludes zero and 7 of 8 traits go the registered way. But the Holm-adjusted Wilcoxon over B1-H1 and B1-H2 is p = 0.055, because n = 8 traits limits it.

**Result: yes, the stopping decision shows up well before `</think>`.**
- On RLVR's A traces the first strong wrap-up signal comes at 22% of the think block. The lag to the realised close is therefore about 78% of the trace, far above the registered 10%.
- **RLVR does not postpone the decision. It signals it earlier than DPO and more strongly, then keeps going.** This holds on every trace source and on D too, more weakly.
- Per word, "So" carries most of the primary mass (on RLVR A traces: RLVR 0.118, DPO 0.097). "Therefore" and "Final" are small.
- **Caveat:** "So" is also a plain connective. The two non-specific proxies ("I'll go with" ≈ 0.05 and "The answer" ≈ 0.04 to 0.05) do not differ much between scorers.

**Verdict (registered reading at the 50% cut on A): the length lives in RLVR's policy, not in the text.** The text share is −0.09, below 0.2.
- **DPO wraps up RLVR's prefixes quickly:** 242 tokens, even fewer than on its own prefixes.

**Verdict: extremity follows the continuing model *and* the text, so the registered reading is not met.**

**Unlike length, which is purely policy (F3), extremity accumulates in the trace as RLVR deliberates.** That fits "deliberation drives the shift". But it also means part of the shift, once written, no longer depends on the RLVR policy.
