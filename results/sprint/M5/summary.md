## Verdict

- **+offset on DPO lengthens A more than D (A/D ratio at α = 1 > 1): no result (CI includes 1)** (1.153 [0.825, 1.606]).
- **−offset on RLVR shortens A (more than D): no result (CI includes 1)** (0.974 [0.614, 1.486]); Δ A think tokens -30 [-464, +366], n = 24, Wilcoxon p = 0.932.
- **Ablating the backtracking direction shortens A: result** (-669 [-1002, -352], n = 24, Wilcoxon p = 7.63e-05); answer change 0.208 vs seed-to-seed 0.191 (registered < 10%).
- **Extremity under the interventions (amendment 5):** ablation Δ readout P(extreme) +0.128 [-0.117, +0.371], n = 24, Wilcoxon p = 0.0457; +offset (α = 1) on DPO +0.184 [-0.013, +0.390], n = 23, Wilcoxon p = 0.0149; −offset (α = 1) on RLVR +0.350 [+0.125, +0.578], n = 22, Wilcoxon p = 0.00167.
- **Answer change under ablation < 10%: failed as registered** (0.208), but the seed-to-seed rate is itself 0.191. The ablation changes answers no more than resampling does.

**Reading (not registered):**
- **Ablating backtracking removes more than half of RLVR's A deliberation** (1175 → 506 tokens, about DPO's 523) without moving the answer beyond seed noise. **But it does not reduce extremity:** P(extreme) stays at or above baseline (+0.13, CI includes 0). At this resolution, the extra deliberation that ablation removes is not what produces the extreme answers.
- **The stage offset is not a clean length knob.** On DPO, +offset lengthens A with dose (523 → 598 → 715 → 1028) and also lengthens D (A/D ratio CI includes 1). On RLVR, −offset does not shorten A at α ≤ 1. Every offset condition, in both directions and on both models, *raises* P(extreme). That pattern fits a generic off-distribution effect on the readout better than a specific deliberation → extremity pathway.
- **Patching RLVR's prompt-position residuals into DPO** at early and middle layers (4, 11, 15) lengthens A (+290 to +350) but not D. P(extreme) rises at layers 15 and 19, so part of RLVR's A-specific state is set at the prompt, before any deliberation.

All M5 contrasts rest on one seed per condition and 24 + 25 items; they are exploratory (amendment 7).
