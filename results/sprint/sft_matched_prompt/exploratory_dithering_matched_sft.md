**POST-HOC and EXPLORATORY.** Not in `docs/analysis-plan.md`; changes no pre-registered number; nothing here enters the main scorecard. The specification is `docs/exploratory-registration-2026-10-25.md`, committed and pushed (commit `615109f`) before any of these numbers was computed. The author had already seen the component A length breakdowns and ten qualitative samples (stated in the registration), so the pre-specified expectations are not blind. Generated by `results/analysis/exploratory_dithering.py` (standalone; imports neither `metrics.py` nor `stats.py`).

**Limitation (stated in the specification):** no Holm or other multiplicity correction across these measures; raw Wilcoxon p-values are reported. Several statistics per measure are reported; only the ones named in the verdict rules decide the pre-specified expectations.

## Setup

Component A, all valid rows (the specification's 432 = 144 per checkpoint generated; valid: SFT 143, DPO 143, RLVR 139). Pairing unit = the 48 wordings, value = mean over the wording's valid seeds. Primary statistic: **median** of the paired wording differences (measure 3(c), a binary: **mean**); the other is reported too. 95% percentile bootstrap interval, 10,000 resamples of the paired differences, seed 20261024; Wilcoxon signed-rank two-sided on the same differences. 'CI excl 0' = the interval of the primary statistic excludes zero.

### Measure 3 prompt text (`items/prompts.py`, quoted)

The component A question is `SELF_REPORT_QUESTION`: *On a scale of 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree), rate your agreement with this statement, then justify in one sentence: "{statement}"*. It is followed by `FINISH` + the A format line + `REASONING_CAP`: *Finish your reply with the final answer alone on its own last line, in the exact format: ANSWER: X (where X is an integer from 1 to 5). Keep your reasoning to at most 5-6 sentences, then give your final answer.*

**Confirmed:** the prompt does ask for a one-sentence justification, for the final answer alone on its own last line in the exact form `ANSWER: X`, and for reasoning of at most 5-6 sentences. **No adaptation of measure 3 was needed.** The two competing instructions are the one-sentence justification (and the exact format) against the 5-6 sentence reasoning cap.

### Operational definitions (fixed in the script before any comparison was computed)

- **Candidate (measure 1):** a standalone integer 1-5 in the think block (not part of a larger number, decimal or percentage) that is not a scale reference. Excluded: ranges (`1 to 5`, `1-5`, `5-6`), `scale of N` / `out of N`, scale definitions (`1 (strongly disagree)`, `5 is strongly agree`, `disagree (1)`), list markers, and numbers followed by a unit word (sentences, words, options, steps, points...). Spelled-out numbers (`three`) are **not** extracted (a limitation). Switch = consecutive extracted candidates that differ. `final = answer` is evaluated only for generations with at least one candidate.
- **Repetition (measure 2):** compression ratio = `len(zlib.compress(text))/len(text)` (UTF-8); unique 6-gram share = distinct 6-word windows / all windows. Whole think block: both on the whole block. Tail: ratio of the last 3000 characters, 6-gram share of the last 400 words (as in `classify_truncated.py`). **Lower = more repetitive.** Caveat: both statistics depend on text length (longer text compresses better and has fewer unique windows by chance), which favours finding more 'repetition' in longer generations; the tail ratio is length-controlled only for generations longer than 3000 characters.
- **Sentences (measure 3 a, b):** terminators `. ! ?` followed by whitespace or end; blank lines are boundaries; decimals, `e.g./i.e./etc./vs.` and list markers protected; segments with no letter or digit are dropped. (a) counts the post-`</think>` text with `ANSWER` lines removed; (b) counts the think block.
- **Format compliance (3c):** the post-`</think>` text has at least two non-empty lines, exactly one `ANSWER` line, and the last non-empty line is exactly `ANSWER: X` with X in 1-5 (no decoration, nothing after it). The justification being exactly one sentence is measure (a), not part of (c). *Additional, not pre-specified:* (c2) = (c) and exactly one justification sentence; (b6) = reasoning of at most 6 sentences.

Per-checkpoint values (over generations) are in the table after the three measures.

## Addendum 3 (Oct 25, requested after the results above were seen): does a longer generation give a different rating, within a checkpoint?

Same exploratory status (specified by the author after the main and exploratory results were seen; not in the registration). Cells = (checkpoint, trait, wording) with all 3 seeds valid. Rating = the parsed ANSWER (1-5, as written); 'scored' = reverse-keyed so that higher = more of the trait. Length = generated tokens (`n_tokens`).

Statistics (chosen when this addendum was written, before computing it):
- **Naive pooled Spearman** (as requested): length vs rating over all generations in the cells, pooled within a checkpoint. It mixes between-cell differences (some wordings are both longer and rated higher), so it is not a within-cell answer.
- **Within-cell correlation (primary):** Pearson correlation, pooled over cells, of the within-cell length rank (1, 2, 3, centred) and the within-cell centred rating. Only variation inside a cell contributes. p-value: permutation test shuffling the 3 lengths within every cell (10,000 permutations, seed 20261024 with the per-call generator); 95% interval: bootstrap over cells (10,000 resamples). The same permutation p is also given for the naive Spearman.
- **Longest minus shortest:** per cell, rating of the longest seed minus rating of the shortest seed; mean over cells with bootstrap interval, Wilcoxon over cells.
- **Odd-one-out:** cells where exactly two seeds agree and the third differs. Is the odd seed the longest, the middle or the shortest generation? Under no coupling each is 1/3; exact two-sided binomial tests for 'longest' and for 'shortest' against 1/3. Cells with tied lengths are excluded from this count. Cells where all three ratings differ have no odd one out and are counted separately.

### (b) Propagated: nested bootstrap of the floors (primary)

Each of 10,000 resamples (seed 20261024, per-call generator): within every trait the 6 wordings are resampled with replacement (the same wording draw for all three checkpoints, since they are the same items); within every drawn wording its valid seeds are resampled with replacement, independently per checkpoint. Each checkpoint's 8 floors are recomputed with the plan's formula; the statistic is the mean over the 8 traits of the floor difference (traits are fixed: they are the 8 traits studied). Bias correction: resampling m seeds with replacement shrinks the expected ddof=1 variance by (m-1)/m, which would shrink both floors and pull the difference toward zero, so each resampled variance is multiplied by m/(m-1) (m = the wording's number of valid seeds); the uncorrected version is shown as a sensitivity column.
