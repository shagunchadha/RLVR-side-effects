**Reading (not registered):**
- **Caution on (2):** because (1) fails (RLVR's own trait directions are only about cos 0.5 from DPO's), a shift along DPO's direction need not mean that RLVR represents *its* trait differently. Part of it can be a generic representational offset between the models.
- The prompt-end CIs are very narrow because the prompt-end projection has no sampling noise: every wording shifts by nearly the same amount, which again points to a model-level offset more than to item-specific effects.
- **Direction of the think-64 shifts:** RLVR sits *lower* than DPO on sycophantic, approval, confidence and composure, and *higher* on hedging and humility, from layer 19 on. That is the opposite of a "more confident persona" account of the extreme-answer shift. Together with M3, the extremity looks built during deliberation rather than carried by a persona shift present at the start of the think block.
