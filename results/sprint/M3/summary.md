**Reading (not registered):** RLVR does not settle early and then pad. Most of its extra tokens (about two thirds) come *before* its answer settles, which is consistent with continued deliberation (F3). During that deliberation P(extreme) climbs steadily, and much more than DPO's: RLVR and DPO start close (+0.04 in the first tenth) and end far apart (+0.15 in the last tenth). The gap opens from the middle of the trace on, and grows more after the wrap-up onset. SFT is the most extreme throughout, as in B8. Probe accuracy at prompt end is low: above the majority baseline only for RLVR (0.39 vs 0.19 at L29), and below it for DPO. So the final answer is weakly decodable before the think block, and much of the extremity is built during it.
