## Engine probe at 32,768 (Nov 28; diagnosis, not a registered job)

`exp/revision/engine_probe.py`, RLVR, F1's AIME load (60 problems × 8 seeds), 10 minutes per variant. Rates are from vLLM's own `generation_tokens` counter, so in-flight tokens are included; the steady window starts after 2 minutes.

| variant | KV cache tokens | max concurrency @ 32,768 | steady tok/s | mean running | tok/s per sequence | max KV usage | preemptions (10 min) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fast` (prefix caching on; the F0b and F1 engine) | 205,391 | 6.27× | 514 | 41.2 | 12.5 | 1.00 | 228 |
| `fast_nopc` (prefix caching off) | 206,047 | 6.29× | 507 | 40.2 | 12.6 | 1.00 | 227 |

- **Speed-up** of `fast_nopc` over `fast`: **0.99×**. The PI's rule needed ≥ 1.5× to act, so **the engine stays `fast`**.
- **The hypothesis is not supported:** turning prefix caching off frees no KV capacity. Both start-up logs report the same cache size and four KV groups.
- **What limits throughput:** the KV cache is full (usage 1.00), and the scheduler pre-empts (about 23 per minute). Per-sequence decode is about 12.5 tok/s at about 41 concurrent sequences.
- **What the logs show:** vLLM 0.31 runs Olmo 3 through its Transformers backend with FlashAttention 2. The KV cache budgets about 368 KiB per token, between the full-attention-only figure (128 KiB) and the all-layers-full figure (512 KiB).
- **About the bench's 319 tok/s:** it is lower than the probe's steady 514 because it includes the run's long tail, when few sequences are left.
